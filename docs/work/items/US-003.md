### US-003: Compute top-10 equal-weight portfolio targets
**As a** portfolio operator, **I want** the 10 publicly-traded clients with the
highest total quarterly lobbying spend (income paid to firms + in-house
expenses) as an equal-weight target portfolio **so that** I can replicate the
"Top Lobbying Spenders" strategy.
**Points:** 3
**Status:** Ready
**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Given cached filings for a quarter, when the signal runs, then each
      client's spend = sum(income) + sum(expenses) across all their filings in
      that quarter.
- [ ] Given ranked spenders, when only mapped (public) companies are available,
      then exactly the top 10 by spend are selected, each at 10% weight.
- [ ] Given fewer than 10 mapped companies, when the signal runs, then weights
      sum to 1.0 across however many are available.
- [ ] Given ties in spend at the cutoff, when selecting, then the tie is broken
      deterministically (alphabetical by client name) and noted in the output.
